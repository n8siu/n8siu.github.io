I'm a PhD candidate in Medical Informatics at UCLA, where I build AI tools that turn medical images into reliable, measurable biomarkers. My dissertation focuses on automating the diagnosis and treatment of limbal stem cell deficiency, a rare, sight-threatening eye disease, using computer vision on corneal microscopy and OCT images.

My work moves from research to practice: I've built and deployed image analysis pipelines that accelerated manual review processes in a Phase I clinical trial, saving hundreds of hours of labor, and run user studies to make sure the tools hold up for clinicians and lab scientists.

**I'm on the job market** for roles starting in late 2026/early 2027 in **medical AI**, **data science**, and **scientific or healthcare consulting**. Feel free to reach out on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nathan-siu/) or download my [CV](/files/NathanSiu_CV.pdf).

What I work on
======
* **Medical imaging AI:** segmentation and quantification for in vivo confocal microscopy, anterior segment OCT, and cultured cell microscopy images
* **Methods:** adapting U-Net, ViT, and Transformer-based methods for medical imaging; NLP and LLM-based methods for unstructured data; agentic tools for automation
* **Tools that get used:** turning research prototypes into validated software for clinical trials, with attention to usability and reproducibility
* **Broader interests:** multimodal medical AI, foundation models, agentic AI, and public health
